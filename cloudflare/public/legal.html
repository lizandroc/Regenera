Accessibility statement

Regenera wants this website to be usable by everyone, including people who use screen readers, keyboard navigation, magnification, or reduced motion. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

What we have done

  • Every page and control works with a keyboard, with a visible focus indicator and a "Skip to main content" link.
  • Headings, landmarks, labels, and text alternatives describe the content for screen readers.
  • A pause button in the header stops all background video and moving content, and the site follows your device's reduced motion setting.
  • Text meets contrast requirements, and the layout adapts to small screens and 200% zoom.
  • Form errors are described in text, announced to assistive technology, and linked to the fields that need attention.
