Regenerative destinations

Ready to be part of something extraordinary?

Regenera brings together distinctive places, aligned capital, operating expertise, and multidisciplinary capabilities to create regenerative wellness destinations where nature, hospitality, culture, community, and well-being come together as a living ecosystem.

How we work

We partner with land and property owners to transform distinctive places into regenerative wellness destinations.

Regenera brings together the strategy, design, capital, operators, and partnerships needed to move from vision to execution. Each project is independently structured while benefiting from the capabilities, relationships, and knowledge of the broader Regenera platform.

The platform

One platform. Multidisciplinary capabilities.

Regenera integrates expertise across regenerative design, real estate development, capital and investment, hospitality, wellness and longevity, technology, operations, brand, culture, and community.

Each destination draws on what it needs while strengthening the platform for what comes next.

The result

Wellness destinations designed to create lasting value for people, nature, communities, and investors.

What we believe

We create places where people and nature thrive together, guided by a simple principle:

Give more than we take.

Inspired by the Mother Tree, Regenera is designed as a living ecosystem where each destination strengthens the next through shared knowledge, relationships, and experience.

  • Stewardship

    Restore and protect the natural systems that sustain each place.

  • Well-being

    Create environments that support vitality, connection, and meaningful experience.

  • Culture

    Honor local identity, traditions, and community.

  • Adaptive design

    Create places that evolve with people, nature, and changing needs.

Wellness

Wellness in the fullest sense

Wellness is not simply an amenity within a Regenera destination. It is embedded in how the entire place is conceived, designed, experienced, and sustained.

We see well-being as a relationship between people, place, purpose, and community. Our destinations support six interconnected dimensions:

01 / 06

Physical

Movement, nutrition, restorative sleep, preventive health, longevity, and connection with nature.

Nature, architecture, food, movement, learning, culture, community, creativity, restoration, and purpose reinforce one another as part of the wellness experience.
